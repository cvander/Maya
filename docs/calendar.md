# Special Dates Calendar

Dates that affect the menu, staffing, or how the room runs. Not every holiday matters. These are the ones that do.

---

## Annual Events

| Date | Event | Impact | Notes |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Jan 1 | New Year's Day | Low volume, recovery crowd | Late open OK. Bloody Mary and brunch vibe if open. |
| Feb (Super Bowl Sunday) | Super Bowl | High volume, early rush | Open early. Beer moves fast. Kitchen if you have one. |
| March 17 | St. Patrick's Day | High volume, menu additions | Irish whiskey specials, extra staff, expect a long night. Green beer is not happening. |
| May (3rd Sunday) | Bay to Breakers | Early crowd, costume chaos | Runners start at 7 AM. Neighborhood bars see overflow by 10 AM. Plan accordingly or don't open early. |
| June (last week) | SF Pride Week | Highest volume week of the year | Plan 2 weeks out. Extra everything. Menu specials welcome. The whole city is out. |
| June (last Sunday) | Pride Parade | Peak single day | All hands on deck. Stock up Thursday. Don't run out of anything. |
| July 4 | Fourth of July | Moderate, neighborhood crowd | Fireworks at the waterfront pull people away. Good night for regulars. |
| Aug (2nd weekend) | Outside Lands | Weekend overflow | Music festival at Golden Gate Park. Expect post-show crowds Friday-Sunday night. |
| Oct (Columbus Day week) | Fleet Week | High volume, tourist heavy | Blue Angels all week. Tourists everywhere. Staff for volume, watch for unfamiliar faces. |
| Oct (1st Saturday) | Hardly Strictly Bluegrass | Weekend overflow | Free festival, Golden Gate Park. Similar to Outside Lands but more chill crowd. |
| Oct 31 | Halloween | High volume, costumes | SF takes this seriously. Expect a long, weird, fun night. Extra staff. |
| Nov 1-2 | Dia de los Muertos | Moderate, cultural | Especially in Mission/Castro. Menu specials with mezcal work well. |
| Dec (2nd Saturday) | SantaCon | High volume, crowd management | The costumes come in waves. Extra staff. Know when to cut people off early. Keep the room from tipping. |
| Dec 31 | New Year's Eve | Highest revenue night | Ticket or no ticket, decide by November. Staff confirmed 2 weeks out. Champagne stocked. |

## How to Use This

- **2 weeks before:** Review staffing, check inventory for themed drinks, plan any menu additions
- **1 week before:** Confirm staff schedule, order extra stock if needed
- **Day of:** Brief staff on the plan, set expectations for the night
- **Day after:** Debrief if anything went sideways

## Adding Events

Add dates here when they earn a spot. Not every holiday needs a plan. If it doesn't change how the bar runs, it doesn't go on the calendar.

Include:
- The date (exact or approximate)
- What it affects (menu, staffing, crowd, all of the above)
- What to watch for
- What NOT to do (just as important)
