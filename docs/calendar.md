# Special Dates Calendar

Dates that affect the menu, staffing, or how the room runs. Not every holiday matters. These are the ones that do.

---

## Annual Events

| Date | Event | Impact | Notes |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| March 17 | St. Patrick's Day | High volume, menu additions | Irish whiskey specials, extra staff, expect a long night. Green beer is not happening. |
| December (2nd Saturday) | SantaCon | High volume, crowd management | The costumes come in waves. Extra staff. Know when to cut people off early. Keep the room from tipping. |

---

## How to Use This

- **2 weeks before:** Review staffing, check inventory for themed drinks, plan any menu additions
- **1 week before:** Confirm staff schedule, order extra stock if needed
- **Day of:** Brief staff on the plan, set expectations for the night
- **Day after:** Debrief if anything went sideways

## Adding Events

Add dates here when they earn a spot. Not every holiday needs a plan. If it doesn't change how the bar runs, it doesn't go on the calendar.

Include:
- The date (exact or approximate)
- What it affects (menu, staffing, crowd, all of the above)
- What to watch for
- What NOT to do (just as important)
