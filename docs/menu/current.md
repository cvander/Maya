# Current Menu

Last updated: 2026-04-08

The menu is not static. It changes based on what we buy out, what's in season, special dates, and what feels right for the room. These are the anchors.

## Cocktails

### Old Fashioned
- 2 oz bourbon (Evan Williams BiB, house pour)
- 1 demerara sugar cube
- 2-3 dashes Angostura bitters
- Orange peel
- Large ice cube

No cherry. No soda. Keep it honest.

**Price:** $14

### Dry Martini
- 2.5 oz gin (Ford's, house pour)
- 0.5 oz dry vermouth (Dolin)
- Lemon twist or olive

Stirred, not shaken. Cold glass.

**Price:** $15

### Manhattan
- 2 oz rye (Rittenhouse BiB)
- 1 oz sweet vermouth (Carpano Antica)
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Luxardo cherry

Stirred, served up.

**Price:** $15

### Margarita
- 2 oz tequila (Espolon Blanco)
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- Salt rim (optional)

Fresh lime only. No sour mix, ever.

**Price:** $14

### Dark & Stormy
- 2 oz rum (Flor de Cana 4yr)
- Fever-Tree ginger beer
- Lime wedge

Simple. Refreshing. Moves well on warm nights.

**Price:** $13

## How the Menu Changes

- **Buy-outs:** When we buy out a product (end of a barrel, last case from a distributor), it goes on special until it's gone
- **Special dates:** Menu additions for events on the [calendar](../calendar.md) -- themed drinks, seasonal pours
- **Season:** Some things only make sense in summer or winter
- **Regulars' picks:** If a trusted advisor brings something worth pouring, it earns a spot

## Adding to the Menu

1. Add the drink here with recipe and price
2. Update the relevant inventory file
3. Note the reason (buy-out, seasonal, special date, regular's pick)
4. Remove it when it's done -- don't let the menu get bloated
