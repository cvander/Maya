### What SFDPH Inspects

- Food handling and storage temperatures
- Handwashing stations functional
- Pest control (evidence of rodents/insects)
- Restroom cleanliness and supplies
- Garbage and recycling handling
- Employee hygiene practices
- Chemical storage (cleaning supplies separated from food/drink)
- Ice machine cleanliness

### What ABC Checks

- License displayed and current
- Hours of operation (no sales after 2:00 AM)
- ID verification practices
- No sales to minors or visibly intoxicated persons
- License conditions being met
- No unauthorized entertainment (if no entertainment permit)

## Notes

- Flag renewals to the lawyer with at least the lead time listed above.
- ABC violations go to the lawyer immediately, no exceptions.
- Keep inspection results on file for 3 years minimum.
- When permits change (new type, new conditions), update this doc same day.
- SFDPH scores are public record. A bad score hurts reputation fast.
- RBS certification is California law since July 2024. No exceptions.
