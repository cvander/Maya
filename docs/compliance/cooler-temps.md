## Rules

- If any reading is above 41F: adjust thermostat, recheck in 1 hour, note corrective action.
- If still above 41F after adjustment: call refrigeration service. Do not serve product from a warm cooler.
- Keep completed weeks on file for 1 year minimum.
- Archive old weeks to `compliance/archive/cooler-temps-YYYY-MM.md`.
